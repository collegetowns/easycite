# Clear Cite

Paste anything you have about a source and get a reference and in-text citation in **APA 7, MLA 9, Chicago 18, Harvard or IEEE** style.

- **Free lookup:** finds journal articles, books, chapters and reports by DOI or title through Crossref. No account needed.
- **Fill it in yourself:** a form that works for any source, including webpages, news stories and videos, even offline.
- **Online analysis:** when opened in Claude, Claude reads whatever you paste and fills in every field.
- Switch styles at any time: the citation and your saved list re-format automatically
- Copy a reference, an in-text citation or the whole list, with italics, to paste into Word or Google Docs
- Download the list as a Word file (with hanging indents) or a text file
- Download the app itself to keep on your computer

Live site: https://clearcite.collegetowns.org
In Claude: https://claude.ai/artifact/PHoUYU82KP7md4gmpEQgfd

## Privacy

Everything runs in the browser. The saved reference list stays in the person's own browser (localStorage). Free lookup sends only the pasted DOI or title to the Crossref API. Online analysis runs only inside Claude, under the person's own Claude account.

## Editing the app

The whole app is the single file `index.html`. It has no build step.
